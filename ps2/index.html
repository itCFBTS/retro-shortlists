PS2 Shortlist

355 PlayStation 2 games worth playing: critically acclaimed, still popular, or loved by the players who rated them. Sorted by how sure we can be that players rate a game highly. Some genres are left out on purpose; see how this list was made.

How this list was made

Of the PS2's 4,338 games, 3,215 are playable without reading Japanese. A few deliberate cuts bring that to 2,625, and a game from those makes this list if any of these is true:

  • Critics rated it 80 or higher.
  • It's among the most popular PS2 games on Vimm's Lair or RetroAchievements.
  • Players rate it highly: a combined user lower bound of 3.9 / 5 or more.

What's deliberately left out (opinionated, so you know what you're not seeing):

  • Japanese-text games (1,123): Japan-only releases that need you to read Japanese, like RPGs, visual novels and text-heavy sims. Import-friendly Japan-only games such as fighters, shooters and racers are kept.
  • Team and league sports (386): American football, soccer, basketball, hockey, baseball, cricket, rugby, volleyball and their management sims. Racing, wrestling, boxing, golf, tennis, pool, fishing and extreme sports stay.
  • Rhythm and music games (198): most need a guitar, drums, a dance mat or a microphone. The peripheral-free ones (Gitaroo Man, Amplitude, Frequency, PaRappa the Rapper 2, Space Channel 5) are left out too, as a matter of taste.
  • Superseded editions (6): when a later release is the same game with more in it, only that one is listed, e.g. Persona 3 FES rather than Persona 3, Virtua Fighter 4: Evolution rather than Virtua Fighter 4.
  • User score: GameFAQs and Metacritic user ratings merged on a /5 scale. Metacritic is converted by matching the two crowds on the same games, and games with few votes are pulled toward an average of 3.5.
  • Lower bound: the score we're ~97.5% confident the game is at least at. A game rated 4.5 by 3,000 people beats one rated 4.5 by six.
  • Votes: GameFAQs plus Metacritic user ratings. Metascore: critic score from Metacritic (blank when Metacritic has none).
